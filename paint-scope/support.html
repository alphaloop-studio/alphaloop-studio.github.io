PaintScope Support

PaintScope helps you mark a wall manually on a room photo and compare paint-color ideas before making a physical sample.

Important color guidance

Screen and photo colors are decision aids. Verify every choice with a physical paint sample under the actual lighting before purchase or application.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I buy Pro? The purchase button stays unavailable when the store does not return the PaintScope Pro product or localized price. Check the network and store account, then try again.

How do I recover Pro? Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the store account that made the original purchase.

Where are my projects? Projects and copied photos are stored only in PaintScope’s app-private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
