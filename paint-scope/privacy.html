PaintScope Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026 · Provider: Alphaloop

Summary

PaintScope does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service, tracking SDK, cloud project service, or remote AI service. Projects and selected room photos are processed and stored locally on your device.

Data PaintScope accesses

When you explicitly choose Own Photo, the operating-system photo picker gives PaintScope access only to the image you select. PaintScope copies that image into app-private storage so you can edit the project offline. The sample room does not request photo access.

Collection and sharing

Alphaloop does not collect or receive your photos, project names, marked wall areas, selected colors, exports, device identifiers, diagnostics, or usage history. PaintScope does not transmit project data to a server operated by Alphaloop.

When you choose to export a decision board, PaintScope opens the operating-system share interface. The destination you select then handles that exported copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

Purchases

PaintScope Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The store may process account and payment information under its own privacy policy. PaintScope receives a platform purchase result and stores only the local Pro entitlement needed to unlock features. Alphaloop does not operate a payment server for PaintScope.

Retention and deletion

Projects, copied photos, palettes, and the local entitlement remain on the device until you delete them or uninstall PaintScope. Deleting a project removes its app-owned project record and copied photo. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data. Copies you exported to another destination must be deleted from that destination separately.

Children

PaintScope is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children.
