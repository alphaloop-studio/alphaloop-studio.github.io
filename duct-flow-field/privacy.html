DuctFlow Field Privacy Policy

Effective: August 13, 2026 · Provider: Alphaloop

Summary

DuctFlow Field does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service, tracking or telemetry SDK, cloud record service, or remote calculation service. Calculations and saved records are processed and stored locally on your device.

Data collection and sharing

Alphaloop does not collect or receive your duct dimensions, airflow targets, calculation results, saved records, exports, device identifiers, diagnostics, or usage history. The app does not transmit calculations or records to a server operated by Alphaloop.

When you choose to export a report image, DuctFlow Field opens the operating-system share interface. The destination you select then handles that exported copy under its own terms and privacy practices.

Purchases

DuctFlow Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The store may process account and payment information under its own privacy policy. DuctFlow Field receives a platform purchase result and stores only the local Pro entitlement needed to unlock features. Alphaloop does not operate a payment server for DuctFlow Field.

Retention and deletion

Saved calculations and the local entitlement remain on the device until you delete them or uninstall DuctFlow Field. You can delete individual saved records inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data. Copies you exported to another destination must be deleted from that destination separately.

Children

DuctFlow Field is a general-audience professional utility and is not directed to children.
