DuctFlow Field Support

DuctFlow Field calculates preliminary airflow capacity, velocity, friction rate, and pressure drop for round, rectangular, and flat-oval duct using standard-air empirical relations.

Important limitation

Results are preliminary and must be checked against current codes, project specifications, manufacturer data, and qualified engineering judgment. DuctFlow Field does not provide certification, engineering approval, final design, or code-compliance assurance.

Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I buy Pro? The purchase button stays unavailable when the store does not return the DuctFlow Pro product or localized price. Check the network and store account, then try again.

How do I recover Pro? Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the store account that made the original purchase.

Where are my records? Records are stored only in app-private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
